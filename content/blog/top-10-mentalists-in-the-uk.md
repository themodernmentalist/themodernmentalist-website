The UK has one of the strongest mentalism scenes in the world, from television names who fill theatres to working performers who bring the same craft into private dinners, weddings and corporate events. This is our own editorial list, which means it is subjective and reflects what we value most: originality, audience reaction, and how well the performance translates to a real room. It is a ranking of style and reputation rather than a measurement.

## 1. Derren Brown

Derren Brown is the UK's best-known mentalist and the performer who changed how a whole generation thinks about the art form. His television specials and long-running theatre shows made psychological illusion, suggestion and misdirection mainstream, and he remains the benchmark every other British mentalist is measured against.

## 2. Chris Cox

Chris Cox is one of the UK's best-known mind readers, with a reputation for fast, polished, audience-driven performances. His presence on screen and stage has helped keep modern mentalism firmly in the public eye.

## 3. Edwin, The Modern Mentalist

Edwin is a London-based mentalist who performs for corporate clients, private events and weddings in the UK and abroad, including destination events. He earned four yeses from the judges on Britain's Got Talent, and his work spans close-up mentalism, a dedicated Magic Booth format, stage shows and hosting. The emphasis is on connection: tailored performances built around the people in the room, designed so guests remember each other as much as the act.

## 4. Shane the Mentalist

Shane the Mentalist began his career as a keynote speaker and brings those skills to his mentalism, communicating with an audience in a way that feels natural, confident and engaging.

## 5. Jamie Nichols

Jamie Nichols is an immersive illusionist whose work blends mentalism and close-up magic into performances that put guests at the centre of the experience.

## 6. Marc Spelmann

Marc Spelmann is a UK-based mentalist who works the private and corporate event circuit.

## 7. Luke Jermay

Luke Jermay is a British mentalist with a strong reputation among other performers for the quality and originality of his material.

## 8. Jon Armstrong

Jon Armstrong is a UK mentalist and mind reader who performs at corporate and private events.

## 9. Jonathan Royle

Jonathan Royle is a British mentalist with a long-running career in performance and in the wider mentalism community.

## 10. Matt Daniel Baker

Matt Daniel Baker is a UK mentalist with a weekly show in London, giving audiences a regular chance to see live mentalism up close.

## How to Choose Between Them

The right performer depends on the job. Television names suit large theatre audiences, while working event mentalists are built for the rooms most people are actually booking for: receptions, dinners, weddings and corporate evenings. ### FAQs

**Who is the best mentalist in the UK?**
Derren Brown is the most famous, but "best" depends on what you need. For a private or corporate event, look for a performer with real audience footage, experience at events like yours, and a tailored approach.

**How do I book a mentalist for an event in London?**
Share your date, venue, guest count and what you want the entertainment to achieve. A good mentalist will reply with a bespoke proposal, confirmed with a deposit and contract.

**Can I book a TV-famous mentalist for a private event?**
Sometimes, though availability and fees are very different from working event mentalists, who perform for private and corporate clients every week.
