There's no single fixed price for hiring a mentalist in London, because the fee depends on the format, the length of the performance, the date, and the size and location of your event. Most experienced mentalists, including performers who work regularly with corporate and UHNW clients, quote per event rather than off a price list.

## What Actually Affects the Price

**Format.** A short, ten-minute show placed within an evening's schedule is a different booking to a Magic Booth running across an entire reception, or a longer stage set built around your event.

**Guest count and format mix.** Close-up work scales with the number of guests it needs to reach; a larger private show for two hundred people is a different job to an intimate dinner for twenty.

**Date and demand.** Peak season dates, particularly weekends in spring and summer for weddings, or December for corporate parties, are in higher demand and priced accordingly.

**Location and travel.** A London event is priced differently to a destination wedding in the Maldives or a private dinner in Miami, once travel and logistics are factored in.

**Bespoke elements.** A performance built around your company's product launch, or personalised to a couple's story, takes more preparation than a standard set, and that shows up in the quote.

## Why Quotes Are Bespoke, Not Fixed

Every event is unique, with its own setting, scale and requirements, so treating pricing as a fixed menu item would misrepresent what's actually being booked. A proper quote follows a short conversation about your date, venue, guest count and what you want the entertainment to achieve, then a bespoke proposal is built around that.

## What to Expect From the Process

1. **Enquiry** — share your date, location and the shape of your event
2. **Proposal** — a tailored quote reflecting your specific requirements
3. **Confirmation** — date secured with a deposit and signed contract
4. **Planning** — a short questionnaire and support in the run-up to the event

### FAQ

**Is a mentalist more expensive than a magician?**
Not inherently, pricing for both is driven more by experience, format and demand than by the discipline itself.

**Do prices change for destination events?**
Yes, travel, accommodation and logistics are added on top of the performance fee for events outside the UK.

**Can I book just a short segment rather than a full evening?**
Yes. A tightly timed show, often around ten minutes, is a common way to add a highlight moment without committing to a longer booking.
