A corporate mentalist is a performer who specialises in entertaining at business events, tailoring a psychological performance to reflect a company's themes, culture and goals rather than delivering a generic show.

## What Sets a Corporate Mentalist Apart

- The ability to customise a performance around your brand or message
- A professional style suited to a business audience
- Skill across close-up, stage and interactive formats
- Familiarity with corporate culture and expectations

## The Role Mentalism Plays at a Corporate Event

Mentalism isn't just a run of tricks, it's a tool. Used well, it can:

- **Break the ice** and encourage networking among guests who don't know each other
- **Reinforce a message**, weaving company values or themes into the performance
- **Create a genuinely memorable moment** attendees talk about after the event
- **Add a lighter beat** to a day of formal discussion, without derailing it

## Formats for Corporate Events

- **Close-up** — the mentalist works small groups directly, ideal for drinks receptions
- **Stage** — a performance built for a larger audience, well suited to keynote slots or after-dinner entertainment
- **Walk-around** — casual, continuous engagement across a trade show floor or exhibition space

Many events combine formats: close-up mentalism during welcome drinks, followed by a stage set later in the evening.

## Customising the Performance for Your Brand

A good corporate mentalist can weave company messaging, product features or even specific values into the performance itself, so the entertainment doesn't just sit alongside your event, it reinforces what the event is actually about.

## Choosing the Right Mentalist

Look for someone who understands the corporate environment: someone who can read a room of executives as easily as a room of graduates, and who tailors the performance rather than repeating the same set regardless of audience. Done well, it's a strategic addition to an event, not just filler between the agenda items.
